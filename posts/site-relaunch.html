Site relaunch — what this space is for

27 May 2026 meta

I've rebuilt the site. The old version was dark and busy — fine for a portfolio, wrong for a research homepage. This one is quieter. The goal is to make the research easier to find and to give myself a place to write.

What I'll write about

Mostly interpretability and AI safety. More specifically: the things I notice while doing research that don't fit neatly into a paper — partial results, open questions, things I'm confused about, things I think are underrated. Occasionally I'll repost something from the Alignment Forum or elsewhere with brief notes on why I found it worth reading.

I won't try to be comprehensive or fast. I'd rather write one good post a month than ten rushed ones.

What's new on the site

  • Publications are updated — ICML 2026 (main track), ACL 2026, two AAAI 2026 papers, and several under review.
  • Talks now includes the AI × Mathematics Residency at ICMS Edinburgh.
  • spectral-trust is on PyPI and actively maintained.
  • The interactive demos are still there — just quieter in the hierarchy now.

How to follow

The simplest option: bookmark this page. No newsletter, no social feed — just a list of posts. If you want to reach me about any of the research, the best way is email.
